Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems Lab (AIIS Lab)
人工智慧與智能系統實驗室

Welcome to the AIIS Lab at National Sun Yat-sen University (NSYSU). Our research focuses on developing cutting-edge AI technologies for complex, real-world challenges.

🚀 Latest News

  • [2026.05] Our paper “CRBF-Net” is accepted by ACM Transactions on Spatial Algorithms and Systems.
  • [2026.04] Lab members presented 3 papers at ICASI 2026 in Kyoto, Japan.
  • [2025.05] Our new research project on Edge AI for Smart Cities is funded by the NSTC.

🧠 Research Interests

We explore the intersection of machine learning and domain-specific applications:

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning 人工智慧與機器學習
  • Feature Selection and Model Compression 特徵選擇與模型壓縮
  • Spatial-Temporal Databases 時空資料庫
  • Smart Manufacturing and IoT 智慧製造與物聯網
  • Databases and Data Mining 資料庫與資料探勘
  • Electronic Game Design and Development 電子遊戲設計與開發
